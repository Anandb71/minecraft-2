A voxel world, ray traced

Minecraft 2

Sixteen kilometres of land built from blocks six centimetres across, every one of them lit by the sun, soaked by the rain, and yours to burn, flood or blow apart.

Play it Watch the world

Rust · wgpu · open source · MIT

No meshes. No baked light. The GPU marches rays through every voxel of the world, so the sunset, the storm, the flood and the fire are all the same thing: light, finding its way.

01 The world

A continent, grown from rock.

Ranges pushed up where plates meet, then eroded by rain into valleys. Strata you can dig through, caves that follow the rock, ten biomes of forest, meadow, desert and snow, and villages and towns built where the ground lets them.

02 Light

Every ray, traced.

Sunlight, skylight, torches and lava are traced through the same voxels you walk on: soft shadows, light that bounces, reflections in polished stone, and glass and water that refract the world behind them.

03 Weather

Rain that soaks in.

Clouds roll in and the wind picks up. Stone darkens, puddles hold the sky, and storms throw real bolts of light that can set a tree burning. Where it is cold, it snows.

04 Fire

Fire that spreads.

Strike flint and steel and it climbs the trunk, takes the canopy and moves downwind, charring wood to charcoal and lighting the night around it. Water puts it out. So does rain, eventually.

05 Water

Water that flows.

A lattice of half-metre cells simulated on the GPU 240 times a second. Break a dam and it floods the square; dig down to the sea and the sea comes in after you. Buckets carry it.

06 Destruction

Everything breaks.

TNT carves real craters and throws real debris that tumbles and stacks where it lands. Weight flows down through every block, so cut the column a tower stands on and the tower comes down.

07 Survival

From one tree to steel.

Punch a log. Planks, sticks, a crafting table, a pickaxe. Forty-four recipes by hand, at a table and in a furnace, tools that wear out, and flint and steel for when you want to watch it burn.

    08 The engine

    Built from the voxel up.

    Everything is written for this game in Rust: the renderer, the physics, the water, the world. Here is what it is made of.

    Voxels
    6.25 cm each, in 4-bit palette bricks under sparse 64-trees. Nothing is ever turned into a mesh.
    World
    16 by 16 km, eroded once by a hydraulic model, streamed around you in four levels of detail.
    Light
    GPU ray marching, ReSTIR direct light from every glowing block, radiance cascades for bounce light, refraction, SVGF denoising, volumetric clouds and fog.
    Water
    Free-surface lattice Boltzmann, D3Q19, stepped at 240 Hz on the GPU and written back into the world.
    Physics
    XPBD rigid bodies at 120 Hz on their own thread, and a load graph that decides what stands.
    Fire & sky
    Block fire at 10 Hz, drifting weather, rain and snow, lightning drawn as voxels of light.
    Quality
    Four tiers, from Realistic up to Super Ultra Crazy Duper Realistic.
    Stack
    Rust 2024, wgpu, WGSL with hot reload, bevy_ecs, its own frame graph. Ten crates and a binary.

    ▶ Play it

    Tonight, on your own GPU.

    You need Rust (stable, 2024 edition) and a graphics card with Vulkan, DirectX 12 or Metal. The first launch grows the world, about thirty seconds; after that it starts at once.

    Terminal 
    git clone https://github.com/Anandb71/minecraft-2.git
cd minecraft-2
cargo run --release
